Privacy Policy

This website is a personal learning project and is provided for educational and demonstration purposes only.

  • The homepage and the plain-text IP endpoint store a private log of that visit: the time, the IP address, the approximate location and ISP details shown on the page, and the browser user agent.
  • Those logs are kept for at most 180 days, then deleted. They are for the site operator only and are not sold or shared.
  • Approximate location comes from an offline IPinfo database on this server. It may be inaccurate, incomplete, or outdated.
  • The information presented here should not be relied upon for security, business, legal, or any other critical purposes.
  • A light or dark theme choice can be saved in this browser only. It is not sent to the server.

By using this website, you acknowledge and agree that it is offered “as-is,” without warranties of any kind, and solely as an educational exercise.
